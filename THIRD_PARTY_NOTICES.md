## FFmpeg and FFprobe

PairCue invokes user-installed FFmpeg/FFprobe for media inspection, subtitle extraction, and PCM
decoding. They are not Python dependencies and are not included in PairCue's source distribution.
FFmpeg may be LGPL or GPL depending on its build configuration. The provided Dockerfile performs a
local source build using Debian's package, which may be GPL-enabled. PairCue does not publish an
official prebuilt image. Review [FFmpeg's legal page](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html) before
distributing a resulting binary or container.

## Network services and content

The OpenSubtitles integration is independently written against the documented OpenSubtitles.com
REST API and requires the user's own API key. OpenSubtitles is a service, not bundled PairCue code.
Its API terms, account limits, and downloaded subtitle content remain subject to the provider's
terms and applicable copyright law.

Translation and transcription endpoints are also user-configured network services. PairCue does
not bundle their models, SDKs, or weights. Enabling transcription sends bounded audio extracts from
the user's media to the configured service.
